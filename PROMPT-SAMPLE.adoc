=== Flight and Procedures

==== Free Flight

===== Day Free Flight

[source,text]
----
Create a daytime **Sinai** **TF-51D** free flight. TF-51D is a **modern retired Mustang** trainer/display aircraft (not WWII combat). Clear light wind; start hot with an AI wingman from a verified Sinai/Suez-area field; fly ~40 minutes on a **plausible VFR/sightseeing route** along canal, coast, and **pyramid/Cairo-direction visual points** within map visibility—follow real corridors, coastline, and common visual landmarks; no random zigzag waypoint spam.

Keep the airspace lived-in but not fake or laggy: military transport/patrol, helicopter transit, light prop or trainers in the pattern, distant non-hostile fighter training—**diverse multi-layer mix**; sparse civil. Types must **match contemporary/display era**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35** (light air + ground); free flight must not go large-scale (hard cap **100** is not a free-flight target). **Spawn & timeline (free flight):** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be airborne on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—later traffic starts from parking/taxi with **staggered takeoffs**, never a simultaneous mass launch. No mandatory waypoints, enemies, or win condition; return, go-around, restart anytime.

Verify era types, fields, realistic routing, staggered timeline, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and recovery brief.
----

===== Night Free Flight

[source,text]
----
Build **Cold War Germany** full-fidelity **MiG-29A** night free flight. Moonlight thin cloud; depart East German runway with a friendly ship; ~35–40 minutes on a **plausible night route** over corridor lights and the border belt (real corridors/field lighting, not random zigzags).

**Diverse but restrained** traffic: transport corridors, helicopter pads, distant friendly night fighter training, ground taxi and lights; sparse civil. Types must **match Cold War East German timeframe**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35**. **Spawn & timeline:** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—ground delayed start, **staggered takeoffs**. No combat, waypoints, or win condition; go-around, divert, restart.

Verify field, night weather, era types, routes, staggered timeline, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and brief.
----

===== Scenic Flight

[source,text]
----
Generate a **Cold War Germany** full-fidelity **MiG-29A** scenic flight as peacetime East German route familiarization / border-corridor VFR (**not hot war, not post-reunification German MiG-29G**). Start hot with a wingman from an East German field; ~45 minutes autumn scattered cloud on a **plausible VFR/sightseeing route** (valleys, corridors, common visual points).

**Diverse** but restrained: friendly transports, low helicopters, non-hostile fighter passes outside ranges, road/field activity; sparse civil. Types must **match Cold War East German timeframe**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35**. **Spawn & timeline:** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—ground delayed start, **staggered takeoffs**. No enemies or win condition; early recovery and relaunch allowed.

Verify map, full-fidelity type, route, staggered timeline, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and route brief.
----

===== Adverse-Weather Flight

[source,text]
----
Make a **Kola** **JF-17** adverse-weather free flight (**real** weather—not light-mist sightseeing). Low cloud, blowing snow/crosswind, limited visibility; cold or hot start with AI wingman from a verified field; ~35–40 minutes on a **plausible coastal/fjord VFR or low IFR-transition route**; early recovery or divert OK. No combat, no win condition.

**Diverse, restrained** traffic: pattern/transport, helicopters, distant non-hostile fighters; sparse civil. Types **contemporary Nordic/training**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35**. **Spawn & timeline:** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—ground delayed start, **staggered takeoffs**.

Check weather, route, era types, staggered timeline, start, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and brief.
----

==== Start and Departure

Departure/procedure training defaults: **full-fidelity types first** (named default seat prefers full-fidelity); plus an **optional aircraft list** (mission slots/F10/client choice—2–4 installed full-fidelity, optionally one FC/free spare; never list unowned types). Support **F10 continuous practice and respawn** (reset lesson, respawn player to parking/runway, reset wingman).

===== Cold Start

[source,text]
----
Build a cold-start exercise at a **Cold War Germany** front-line field. **Default seat: full-fidelity MiG-29A**; **optional list** (verify installed): full-fidelity M-2000C, JF-17, F-16C, etc. Summer morning: power-up, avionics, taxi, takeoff, join same-type for ~20 min local route. Light traffic. Abort start/go-around OK; no enemies.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 at least: Reset cold-start lesson, **respawn player to parking**, reset wingman, re-run power-up. Continuous starts; do not force-end after one departure.

Verify parking, taxi, radios, routes, aircraft list, F10; beginner brief and validate.
----

===== Hot Start

[source,text]
----
Build a hot-start / local departure exercise on **Iraq** (full or **Iraq North**). **Default seat: full-fidelity F-16C** (or full-fidelity M-2000C—state default); **optional list** may include F-100D, JF-17, full-fidelity MiG-29A if installed. Hot morning haze; depart verified military field with AI wingman; navigation, formation, local recovery; high friendly transport; no enemies.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 reset sortie, hot-start player respawn, reset wingman; continuous local work.

Confirm map, start, list support, AI routes, F10; brief and validate.
----

[source,text]

Build **Caucasus** runway takeoff / climb-profile exercise. **Default full-fidelity Su-25T**; **optional list**: full-fidelity M-2000C, JF-17, A-10C II, etc. Overcast morning; climb profile, training waypoints, follow instructor home; transport departs after you. Go-around or divert OK; no enemies.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 reset takeoff lesson, respawn to runway/taxi, reset instructor; continuous profiles.

Verify runway, start, traffic timing, aircraft list, F10; procedure notes and validate.

==== Navigation and Landing

Landing training defaults: **full-fidelity first** + **optional aircraft list** + **F10 continuous approaches/respawn** (go-around, re-approach, respawn to downwind/final, optional wind reset).

===== Visual Landing

[source,text]
----
Generate daytime visual landing on Caucasus. **Default full-fidelity F-16C** (good pattern FOV); **optional list**: full-fidelity M-2000C, JF-17, full-fidelity MiG-29A, F/A-18C. Air-spawn ~15 nm; join behind same-type instructor; light traffic; full stop, touch-and-go, second approach, or divert.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 at least: Reset approach, **respawn player to downwind/15 nm**, reset instructor, another full-stop. Continuous visual work; do not force-end after one landing.

Verify field, approach, aircraft list, traffic, F10; approach card and validate.
----

===== Instrument Landing

[source,text]
----
Build **Kola** instrument landing. **Default full-fidelity JF-17**; **optional list**: full-fidelity M-2000C, F-16C, full-fidelity MiG-29A, F/A-18C. Start above layer; low cloud, light snow, crosswind; follow transport; verified onboard/field aids; no combat; fuel for go-around/divert.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 reset instrument approach, respawn to above-layer/procedure start, fly ILS/LOC again (as map supports). Continuous instrument work.

Verify aids, weather, aircraft list, F10; procedure brief and validate.
----

==== Formation Flight

[source,text]
----

Allow rejoin or solo go-around if separated.

==== Carrier Operations

[source,text]
----
Build a **Marianas** **F-14** carrier launch-and-recovery mission, two-crew capable (Pilot/RIO). **Variants:** Heatblur full-fidelity family includes **F-14A**, **F-14B**, and expansion **F-14B(U)** (2000s modernized; requires base A/B or Ultimate). Verify **Supercarrier** if required.

Confirm carrier, deck, variant IDs, and recovery data; build and validate a .miz and deck brief.

[[bfm-acm]]
=== Close Air Combat (BFM/ACM)

*Grindable sparring* (modern guns/IR/BVR): **full-fidelity first** · **red/blue side choice** · **common-type roster** · **F10 continuous sparring/respawn**.  
*One-shot ordinary missions* (WWII bomber, furball, travelers): fixed script—**no** infinite F10 wave requirement.

==== Guns

===== Modern Guns 1v1 (Red/Blue · Grindable)

[source,text]
----
Build a **modern standard** guns-only 1v1 **grindable** training (not WWII). Map **Nevada** (or CWG/Caucasus—state in brief).

**Red/Blue:** Provide **Blue / Red** choice (client slots or F10 side select); swapping sides swaps player and bandit type pools.  
**Common roster** (installed only; full-fidelity preferred; FC as adversary/spare OK):  
- Blue/West: full-fidelity F-16C, F/A-18C, F-14B, F-15E, M-2000C, F-4E, A-10C II, AV-8B; FC F-15C, F-5E FC  
- Red/East: full-fidelity MiG-29A, JF-17, MiG-21Bis, AJS-37; FC MiG-29S, MiG-29G, Su-27, J-11A, Su-25T  
**Default example:** Blue F-16C vs Red F-5E FC (or Red full-fidelity MiG-29A vs Blue F-16C). **Guns only**, no missiles; equal-energy head-on; no out-of-bounds or shots at disengaged/chutes.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 at least: pick/swap side, Reset geometry, Next Wave, Respawn Player, Respawn Bandit. Infinite sparring; do not force-end after one round. ~8–10 min per round.

Verify roster, red/blue slots, guns, F10; training brief and validate.
----

===== WWII Bomber Intercept (One-Shot)

[source,text]
----
Build a **one-shot ordinary mission**: WWII bomber guns intercept (not grindable).  
**Asset fact:** DCS **WWII Assets** heavy bombers are essentially **US/Allied**—this mission intercepts an **American bomber stream** (do not invent German heavy bombers).  
**Default player Germany:** two-ship **Bf 109 K-4** or **Fw 190 A-8** (player lead; lock one or offer both as seats) intercepts escorted US bombers from a continental/forward field; escort may be installed Allied fighters (**P-51D** / **P-47D** / **Spitfire**). Map **The Channel** or **Normandy** (prefer 2.0). **Guns primary**; 1944 dusk thin cloud; break/kill bombers and recover—ends. No friendly/neutral shots. No infinite F10 waves.

Verify WWII bomber units, German fighters, escort, bounds; brief and validate.
----

===== WWII Multi-Aircraft Furball (One-Shot)

[source,text]
----
Build a **one-shot ordinary mission**: WWII multi-aircraft guns furball (not grindable). **Caucasus** or **Marianas WWII**: player **La-7** or **F4U-1D**; airspace holds **Fw 190 A-8**, **Fw 190 D-9**, **Bf 109 K-4**, and friendly props; **guns only**; ~12–15 min one bout, score survival/kills then end. Active **≤35**, staggered entry. Report La-7 EA limits. No infinite F10 wave respawn.

Verify type pool and bounds; furball brief and validate.
----

BVR *grindable* training: **full-fidelity first** · **red/blue side choice** · **common-type roster** · **F10 continuous sparring/respawn**.

[source,text]
----

[[quick-training]]
== Quick Training

Immediate, short practice and brief engagements: free flight, cold starts, procedures, and compact air fights that end when the sortie ends.

=== Free Flight

==== Day Free Flight

[source,text]
----
Create a daytime **Sinai** **TF-51D** free flight. TF-51D is a **modern retired Mustang** trainer/display aircraft (not WWII combat). Clear light wind; start hot with an AI wingman from a verified Sinai/Suez-area field; fly ~40 minutes on a **plausible VFR/sightseeing route** along canal, coast, and **pyramid/Cairo-direction visual points** within map visibility—follow real corridors, coastline, and common visual landmarks; no random zigzag waypoint spam.

Keep the airspace lived-in but not fake or laggy: military transport/patrol, helicopter transit, light prop or trainers in the pattern, distant non-hostile fighter training—**diverse multi-layer mix**; sparse civil. Types must **match contemporary/display era**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35** (light air + ground); free flight must not go large-scale (hard cap **100** is not a free-flight target). **Spawn & timeline (free flight):** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be airborne on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—later traffic starts from parking/taxi with **staggered takeoffs**, never a simultaneous mass launch. No mandatory waypoints, enemies, or win condition; return, go-around, restart anytime.

Verify era types, fields, realistic routing, staggered timeline, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and recovery brief.
----

==== Night Free Flight

[source,text]
----

**Diverse but restrained** traffic: transport corridors, helicopter pads, distant friendly night fighter training, ground taxi and lights; sparse civil. Types must **match Cold War East German timeframe**. **Performance & scale:** active units typically **≤35**. **Spawn & timeline:** only at **t=0** may a few aircraft already be on routes; after that **no mid-air spawns**—ground delayed start, **staggered takeoffs**. No combat, waypoints, or win condition; go-around, divert, restart.

Verify field, night weather, era types, routes, staggered timeline, and total unit count; build and validate a .miz and brief.
----

=== Procedures

==== Start and Departure

Departure/procedure training defaults: **full-fidelity types first** (named default seat prefers full-fidelity); plus an **optional aircraft list** (mission slots/F10/client choice—2–4 installed full-fidelity, optionally one FC/free spare; never list unowned types). Support **F10 continuous practice and respawn** (reset lesson, respawn player to parking/runway, reset wingman).

===== Cold Start

[source,text]
----
Build a cold-start exercise at a **Cold War Germany** front-line field. **Default seat: full-fidelity MiG-29A**; **optional list** (verify installed): full-fidelity M-2000C, JF-17, F-16C, etc. Summer morning: power-up, avionics, taxi, takeoff, join same-type for ~20 min local route. Light traffic. Abort start/go-around OK; no enemies.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 at least: Reset cold-start lesson, **respawn player to parking**, reset wingman, re-run power-up. Continuous starts; do not force-end after one departure.

Verify parking, taxi, radios, routes, aircraft list, F10; beginner brief and validate.
----

Hot morning haze; depart verified military field with AI wingman; navigation, formation, local recovery; high friendly transport; no enemies.

**Training loop (F10 / respawn):** F10 reset sortie, hot-start player respawn, reset wingman; continuous local work.

Confirm map, start, list support, AI routes, F10; brief and validate.

[source,text]

== Mission Scenarios

Typical single-mission combat: one brief, one package, one ending. Not infinite-range training.

=== One-Shot Air Combat

==== WWII Bomber Intercept (One-Shot)

[source,text]
----
Build a **one-shot ordinary mission**: WWII bomber guns intercept (not grindable).  
**Asset fact:** DCS **WWII Assets** heavy bombers are essentially **US/Allied**—this mission intercepts an **American bomber stream** (do not invent German heavy bombers).  
**Default player Germany:** two-ship **Bf 109 K-4** or **Fw 190 A-8** (player lead; lock one or offer both as seats) intercepts escorted US bombers from a continental/forward field; escort may be installed Allied fighters (**P-51D** / **P-47D** / **Spitfire**). Map **The Channel** or **Normandy** (prefer 2.0). **Guns primary**; 1944 dusk thin cloud; break/kill bombers and recover—ends. No friendly/neutral shots. No infinite F10 waves.

Verify WWII bomber units, German fighters, escort, bounds; brief and validate.
----

[source,text]

== Quick Missions

=== Flight and Procedures

==== Free Flight

===== Day Free Flight

[source,text]
----
Create a daytime Caucasus F-16C free flight. Start hot with an AI wingman at a coastal field and fly about 30 minutes in clear, light-wind weather. Add light civil and airport traffic but no enemies, mandatory waypoints, or win condition; allow return, go-around, or restart anytime.

Check field, traffic, and routes; build a .miz and brief, validating separation and recovery.
----

===== Night Free Flight

[source,text]
----
Build a Persian Gulf full-fidelity M-2000C night free flight. Depart with a friendly aircraft and fly about 35 minutes over the city and coast under moonlight and thin cloud. Keep one neutral airliner safely separated, add no combat or compulsory action, and allow go-around or diversion.

After checking field, night weather, and routes, build and validate a .miz and brief.
----

===== Scenic Flight

[source,text]
----
Generate a Cold War Germany MiG-29G sightseeing flight as a post-reunification German exchange sortie. Start hot with a wingman at a western field and fly about 45 minutes in scattered cloud on an autumn afternoon, observing a friendly transport and civil traffic. Add no enemy or win condition; permit early recovery and another departure.

Verify terrain, aircraft, and waypoints, then build and validate a .miz and route brief.
----

===== Adverse-Weather Flight

[source,text]
----
Make a Kola adverse-weather free flight for the full-fidelity MiG-29A. Cold-start at dawn with an AI wingman and fly about 40 minutes through low cloud, snow, and strong crosswind. Keep a friendly transport above the layer and base traffic active; add no combat objective and allow early recovery or diversion.

With weather, route, and start state checked, build and validate a .miz and instrument brief.
----

==== Start and Departure

===== Cold Start

[source,text]
----
Create a JF-17 cold-start exercise at Nellis on the Nevada map. On a summer morning, power up, prepare avionics, taxi, take off, then join a friendly JF-17 for a 20-minute local route. Keep light ground and air traffic; allow an aborted start, go-around, or restart.

Use checked parking, taxi, radios, and routes to build and validate a .miz and beginner brief.
----

===== Hot Start

[source,text]
----
Give me a Marianas F-15C hot-start mission. On a tropical morning, depart a land base with an AI wingman and spend about 35 minutes practicing navigation, formation changes, and recovery in scattered cloud and light wind. Add a friendly high patrol and airport transport; allow early separation or go-around.

Confirm terrain, spawns, and AI routes; produce and validate a .miz and brief.
----

===== Runway Takeoff

[source,text]
----
Build a Caucasus Su-25T runway-takeoff exercise. On an overcast morning, follow a specified climb profile, pass a training-area waypoint, and recover behind a friendly trainer in about 25 minutes. Launch a transport after the player clears the field; permit immediate return, go-around, or diversion.

Use Docs and Tools to check runway, start, and traffic timing, then build and validate a .miz and notes.
----

==== Navigation and Landing

===== Visual Landing

[source,text]
----
Create a full-fidelity M-2000C daytime visual-landing exercise. Start airborne about 15 nautical miles from a Caucasus field behind a same-type instructor joining the pattern, with light friendly traffic. In good weather and light wind, land full-stop within about 20 minutes, or fly a touch-and-go, another approach, or a diversion.

Check field, approach, and traffic; build a .miz and card, validating spacing and go-arounds.
----

===== Instrument Landing

[source,text]
----
Build a JF-17 instrument-landing exercise in the Caucasus. Start above the layer and follow a friendly transport through low cloud, light rain, and haze using verified aircraft and field facilities. Keep the roughly 25-minute mission noncombat with fuel for a missed approach, another attempt, or weather diversion.

Create and validate a .miz and brief from verified aids, weather, and approach data.
----

===== Night or Crosswind Landing

[source,text]
----
Generate an F-16C night crosswind landing on Sinai. Start downwind with moderate fuel, conspicuous gusts, and usable visibility; put a friendly F-16C ahead and a neutral flight outside the approach sector. Within about 25 minutes, allow go-around and another approach or diversion to a less windy field.

Confirm runway, wind, and both routes before building and validating a .miz and night brief.
----

==== Formation Flight

[source,text]
----
Generate a Nevada four-ship F-5E FC formation exercise; the player is number two. Start hot near Nellis and spend about 45 minutes in scattered morning cloud and mild turbulence practicing join-up, formation changes, tactical turns, and the recovery break. Keep a friendly tanker and airport traffic nearby; allow regrouping or a solo go-around.

Build and validate a .miz and formation brief from checked aircraft, AI, and airspace.
----

==== Air-to-Air Refueling

[source,text]
----
Create a Persian Gulf F/A-18C refueling exercise. Start airborne with a wingman, follow controller calls to the tanker, and practice rendezvous, transfer, and breakaway for about 40 minutes in calm daylight. Separate a civil airway and shipping, allow repeated rejoins, and retain fuel for carrier recovery or land diversion even without a transfer.

Build and validate a .miz and brief after checking tanker compatibility, route, and carrier; flag unsupported items.
----

==== Carrier Operations

[source,text]
----
Build a Marianas F-14B carrier exercise for an optional two-seat crew. Start hot on deck, launch behind a friendly F-14B, fly a short route, and make a clear-afternoon Case I recovery in about 35 minutes. Keep a rescue helicopter and patrol aircraft outside the pattern, add no combat, and allow bolter or land diversion.

Confirm carrier, deck, and recovery, then build and validate a .miz and deck brief.
----

[source,text]
----
Build a Mi-24P nap-of-the-earth exercise in Syria. Start hot with a friendly Mi-8 at a forward field and fly about 35 minutes through valleys, around villages, and behind terrain in light morning haze and wind. Observation posts only referee and never engage; allow following the friendly aircraft, choosing a route, early recovery, or restart after landing.

Check terrain, units, and exercise rules, then build and validate a .miz and route brief.
----

===== Confined-Area Landing

[source,text]
----
Generate a CH-47F confined-area landing exercise in Afghanistan. Depart a forward base with a light friendly helicopter and attempt three increasingly difficult valley sites in hot-afternoon mountain gusts at a safe weight. Keep the 45-minute mission enemy-free and allow a go-around, retry, skipped site, or alternate landing.

Build and validate a .miz and site card from checked slope, weight, and weather.
----

=== Close Air Combat (BFM/ACM)

==== Guns

===== 1v1

[source,text]
----
Create a Caucasus guns-only full-fidelity M-2000C versus full-fidelity MiG-29A 1v1; either side is playable. After instructors leave, begin head-on at equal speed and medium altitude. Clear-weather rounds last about 8 minutes, permit only guns and no boundary pursuit, and end with a kill or forced disengagement; loss, low fuel, or exit resets the original geometry.

Check aircraft, guns, and boundaries; build a .miz and dual brief, validating scoring and reset.
----

===== 2v2

[source,text]
----
Build a Nevada guns-only F-5E FC versus MiG-21Bis 2v2. Start head-on at medium altitude with the player leading evenly matched AI; keep the controller and civil airway outside. Clear-weather rounds last about 10 minutes, forbid missiles, boundary pursuit, and attacks on disengaging aircraft, and end when one side loses combat ability or escapes. Allow restart or side swap.

Confirm aircraft, guns, and airspace, then build and validate a .miz and red-blue rules card.
----

===== 1v2

[source,text]
----
Generate a guns-only F-16C versus two F-5E FCs 1v2. Give the fuel-limited player height while two medium-skill AI enter spread below and a controller stays outside. Run 10-minute rounds with no missiles, boundary crossing, or controller attack; prioritize energy management and survival, make both kills optional, and allow disengagement or reset.

Generate and validate a .miz and training brief from checked loads, geometry, and fuel.
----

==== Infrared Missiles

===== 1v1

[source,text]
----
Create a Marianas offshore full-fidelity MiG-29A versus F/A-18C infrared 1v1. Begin head-on at visual range and medium altitude, with range control and a naval group outside. Give each aircraft exactly two short-range infrared missiles plus guns, but no radar missiles. Engage only after positive identification; an 8-minute round ends with a kill or safe withdrawal, with reset, side swap, and recovery available.

Produce and validate a .miz and rules brief after checking weapons, geometry, and range units; flag unsupported items.
----

===== 2v2

[source,text]
----
Generate a 1980s Nevada Red Flag infrared Mirage F1 versus F-5E FC 2v2. The player leads; a controller only calls fight-on and terminate. Give each aircraft a period-appropriate limited infrared-and-gun load. Run 12-minute rounds, forbid attacks on disengaging aircraft or ejected crews, score formation survival and coordination, and allow reset or side swap.

Verify period aircraft, weapons, and airspace; build and validate a .miz with briefs for both sides.
----

===== 1v2

[source,text]
----
Build a close engagement pitting the full-fidelity MiG-29A against two F-16Cs in a 1v2. Give the player two infrared missiles and guns; bring opponents from separate bearings slightly below, with a controller outside. Scattered cloud and low evening sun complicate the 10-minute visual-search, threat-prioritization, and disengagement exercise. Prohibit boundary pursuit; allow reset or recovery.

Use checked weapons, geometry, and AI timing to build and validate a .miz and learning-objectives card.
----

==== Fully Armed Surprise Encounter

[source,text]
----
Create a fully armed Kola encounter between two F-14Bs and two MiG-29Ss. Give both full air-combat loads and place the formations above and below low cloud for short-range discovery. AWACS communications resume after fighting begins, a neutral patrol needs protection, and the roughly 20-minute problem permits combat, covering withdrawal, or recovery. AI identifies before firing; survival and identification outrank kills.

Confirm loads, AWACS, and neutrals, then build and validate a .miz and situation brief.
----

=== Beyond-Visual-Range Combat (BVR)

==== 1v1

[source,text]
----
Generate a Persian Gulf F-16C versus JF-17 BVR 1v1. Start about 45 nautical miles apart at similar energy with medium-range and self-defense weapons. AWACS gives limited information; civil traffic and shipping stay outside. In 15-minute rounds, forbid support attacks and boundary pursuit; accept a kill followed by disengagement or safe recovery from disadvantage, and allow side swap and reset.

Produce and validate a .miz and channel card after checking weapons, AWACS, and neutral routes; flag unsupported items.
----

==== 2v2

[source,text]
----
Build a Marianas offshore F-14B versus full-fidelity MiG-29A BVR 2v2. Start parallel formations about 55 nautical miles apart with combat loads and intermittent cloud masking; each side has a nonparticipating controller. Run 18-minute rounds on spacing, target sorting, mutual support, and post-launch defense. Forbid boundary pursuit; allow damaged-aircraft recovery, reset, or side swap.

After checking loads, geometry, and controllers, build and validate a .miz and tactical brief.
----

==== 1v2

[source,text]
----
Create a Caucasus mountain-edge full-fidelity M-2000C versus two lower-skill J-11A BVR 1v2. Let the player choose height and speed with semi-active radar weapons; opponents bracket loosely, ground control gives intermittent bearings, and a neutral transport must be avoided. Within about 18 minutes, require one disciplined attack and safe disengagement; allow terrain-assisted recovery or reset.

Check sensors, bracket AI, and neutral routes; build and validate a .miz and control brief.
----

==== 4v4 with AWACS and Datalink

[source,text]
----
Generate a Sinai multinational-exercise JF-17 versus Su-27 BVR 4v4. Give both sides airborne warning; let the player formation use supported datalink for target allocation and mutual support. A neutral airway skirts the area in thin daytime cloud. Run 25-minute rounds; forbid attacks on support, neutral, or exited aircraft; score formation survival, identification, and safe recovery; allow reset or side swap.

Build and validate a .miz and control brief after checking weapons, datalink, and AWACS; flag unsupported items.
----

==== Defensive Disengagement

[source,text]
----
Build a JF-17 defensive-disengagement exercise near a Kola fjord. Start alone, tracked by two enemies as a long-range missile threat develops; AWACS calls intermittently and a low-fuel friendly recovers behind. In low cloud, light snow, and limited fuel, do not return fire; use awareness, defenses, terrain, and energy to reach a safe corridor within about 15 minutes. Allow reset or continued recovery.

From checked defenses, threat timing, and friendly routes, create and validate a .miz and escape card.
----

=== Air-to-Ground Weapons Training

==== Cannon Strafing

[source,text]
----
Create a Su-25T cannon range. Start airborne with a nonattacking observer and attack stationary trucks, light armor, and marked targets along the published axis on a clear morning. Targets do not fire back; protect a friendly convoy and maintenance area outside. Allow repeated passes for about 30 minutes; an unsafe pass ends only that run, ammunition exhaustion prompts recovery, and restart restores targets.

With cannon, targets, and safety zones confirmed, produce and validate a .miz and range brief.
----

==== Rockets

[source,text]
----
Build a Sinai Mi-24P rocket exercise. Depart a forward site with a friendly Mi-8, follow a safe corridor, and wait for an observer to open the range before attacking light vehicles and area targets. Protect settlements and friendly positions in hot-afternoon light crosswind. Use the rockets for repeated passes during about 35 minutes; entering a safety zone aborts only that pass, with retry or recovery available.

Confirm rockets, range, and observer, then build and validate a .miz and safety brief.
----

==== CCIP Unguided Bombs

[source,text]
----
Generate an F-16C CCIP unguided-bomb exercise from Nellis. Start hot with a friendly F-16C and attack varied circular targets in clear, light-crosswind weather. Range control opens shallow and steeper delivery windows; friendly vehicles outside require the published axis. Allow repeated deliveries for about 40 minutes; a miss is not failure, and the player may re-enter or recover.

Use checked bombs, targets, and safety zones to build and validate a .miz and delivery card.
----

==== CCRP Unguided Bombs

[source,text]
----
Create a full-fidelity M-2000C CCRP exercise on Cold War Germany. Depart with a same-type wingman, ingress low in autumn overcast and moderate wind, and make a level delivery on a simulated runway. A friendly transport uses the adjacent block; no interceptor appears. Use compatible unguided bombs in a 35-minute mission; if release conditions fail, overfly, rebuild, retry, and recover.

After checking bombs, designation, and routes, build and validate a .miz and delivery brief.
----

==== Cluster Munitions

[source,text]
----
Generate a Caucasus F-16C cluster-munition exercise. Start airborne, contact range control, and compare two safe release altitudes against dispersed soft targets and light armor. Targets do not fire back; keep friendlies, settlements, and a neutral road clear while wind shifts the axis. In about 30 minutes, score effects and rule compliance separately; entering an exclusion area aborts only that run, allowing reattack or recovery.

Build and validate a .miz and weapons brief from verified stores, wind, and safety zones.
----

==== Laser-Guided Weapons

[source,text]
----
Build a Marianas F/A-18C laser-guided weapons exercise. Start hot on a carrier with a wingman; in scattered afternoon cloud, let a JTAC sequentially designate two island targets while protecting a nearby friendly convoy. Spend about 45 minutes on contact, identification, delivery, and egress. If communications or laser support fails, rebuild the attack; then recover aboard or divert ashore.

Generate and validate a .miz and attack brief after checking weapon, laser, and JTAC support; flag unsupported items.
----

==== Television or Electro-Optical Weapons

[source,text]
----
Generate a Caucasus JF-17 electro-optical weapons exercise. Start hot and receive reconnaissance on a valley radar vehicle and simulated bridge, with a friendly patrol nearby. Weather permits sensor use, but cloud shadow and terrain intermittently mask both targets. In about 40 minutes, choose priority and score one effective hit before egress; after failed search or delivery, reacquire, reattack, or recover.

Check weapon, sensor, and targets, then build and validate a .miz and intelligence brief.
----

==== GPS/INS-Guided Weapons

[source,text]
----
Build a Sinai F/A-18C GPS/INS-guided weapons exercise. Depart a land base with a wingman on a clear morning and attack three targets at briefing coordinates without entering a simulated short-range threat ring. Protect friendlies and a neutral road. During about 45 minutes, report each aimpoint result; after a data-entry or delivery error, use remaining weapons to rebuild the route and recover.

Generate and validate a .miz and coordinate card using checked stores, coordinates, and threat ring.
----

==== Standoff Weapons

[source,text]
----
Generate a Marianas JF-17 standoff exercise. Start airborne with an escort and reconnaissance updates on a hardened coastal command post behind a medium-range defense; neutral shipping limits launch direction. In a cloudy 45-minute evening mission, practice outside-ring routing, confirmation, launch timing, and fast egress. If the window is missed, allow one new setup before recovery or diversion.

After confirming weapon, defense, and neutral shipping, produce and validate a .miz and mission card.
----

Build a Nevada F-16C anti-radiation exercise. After departure, a friendly electronic-support aircraft reports several radars cycling by schedule. Other ground units remain in training behavior; unconfirmed friendly simulators cannot be attacked. In about 40 minutes, identify threats, choose a supported method, and suppress at least one emitter; wait or re-enter after a missed window, then recover.

Generate and validate a .miz and threat brief after checking weapon, radar AI, and suppression logic; flag unsupported items.

In low cloud and gusty sea wind, use anti-ship weapons for a coordinated release and fast escape during about 45 minutes, with no hostile fighters. Recover after a primary hit, convoy abort, or inability to launch.

Using verified weapons, ships, and neutral routes, build and validate a .miz and identification brief.

==== Radar, IRST, and Datalink

[source,text]
----
Generate a full-fidelity MiG-29A sensor exercise in Cold War Germany airspace. Include friendly MiG-29As, adversary trainers, neutral transports, and civil traffic while ground control gives limited clues. In thin afternoon cloud for about 35 minutes, classify contacts with radar, IRST, and datalink functions, keeping weapons hold. A mistake permits another look; finish with recovery or an exercise reset.

Confirm sensors, routes, and coalitions, then build and validate a .miz and classification card.
----

==== Targeting Pod and JTAC

[source,text]
----
Build a Sinai A-10C II pod-and-JTAC exercise. Start hot with an observing wingman, then receive a nine-line, verified coordinates, and laser cues for vehicles, a simulated point beside a structure, and a moving column. Protect friendlies and a neutral road. During a clear-morning exercise of about 45 minutes, search, mark, and dry-run without live weapons; allow repeated calls, reacquisition, and recovery.

Use verified pod, JTAC, and laser support to create and validate a .miz and nine-line brief.
----

==== RWR, ECM, and Countermeasures

[source,text]
----
Generate a full-fidelity M-2000C defensive-systems exercise off the Marianas. Along a fixed route, encounter search radar, tracking radar, and a safely simulated missile while a friendly instructor observes and neutral traffic stays clear. In scattered evening cloud for about 30 minutes, use RWR, ECM, chaff, and flares without counterattack. Reset a failed stage in the safe area, then recover.

With defenses, threats, and simulation checked, build and validate a .miz and stage card.
----

==== Low-Level Penetration

[source,text]
----
Build a Cold War Germany MiG-29G low-level penetration. Follow river valleys, forest edges, and low ground to a simulated target without releasing weapons. Friendly reconnaissance monitors above, training radars only record exposure, and neutral roads and towns are excluded. In an overcast, moderately windy 35-minute run at dawn, score routing, exposure, timing, and safe recovery; a deviation permits rejoin.

Verify terrain, radar, and exclusion zones, then create and validate a .miz and low-level card.
----

==== Emergency Recovery

[source,text]
----
Create a Marianas F-14B emergency recovery. During a two-seat patrol with a wingman, have one engine fail together with a hydraulic-system fault, but only if both are supported. The wingman escorts, the carrier recovers another friendly aircraft, a land alternate remains open, and weather turns from clear to scattered cloud. In about 30 minutes, manage jettison, calls, and fuel; choose a safe trap, bolter and retry, or diversion; score only crew and aircraft safety.

Generate and validate a .miz and emergency card after checking failures, jettison, and carrier AI; flag unsupported items.
----

== Combat Scenarios

=== Air Superiority and Air Defense

==== Scramble Interception

[source,text]
----
Generate a summer 1988 full-fidelity MiG-29A scramble on Cold War Germany. Player and AI wingman cold-start at an East German base as M-2000C-escorted Mirage F1 strikers approach a military target near East Berlin under low cloud; A-50 and ground control provide tracks but do not fight. Intercept before weapons release, then recover home or alternate within 70 minutes; verify project data and deliver the .miz, briefing, and validation report.
----

==== Strike-Package Interception

[source,text]
----
Build a dusk Persian Gulf interception for two carrier-launched F-14Bs. Under E-2 control beside fleet CAP, players identify long-range bombers and fighter escort while a neutral airliner strays near the engagement area; a low-level decoy may split attention. Engage only confirmed hostiles, disrupt the bomber raid, and trap or divert within 85 minutes; validate identification and carrier recovery in the .miz.
----

==== BARCAP

[source,text]
----
Generate two-player J-11A BARCAP over the Mariana Islands in poor weather to protect a frontline airfield, supply flight, and coastal radar. Players relieve a low-fuel Su-27 patrol for a 60-minute station with limited AWACS and tanker support; two hostile waves may divide between base and transport. Hold until relief and protect both; build the .miz from checked data and validate fuel, routes, and threat logic.
----

==== TARCAP

[source,text]
----
Create a four-ship M-2000C TARCAP on Sinai. Launch from a rear base at dawn ahead of a friendly JF-17 precision-strike package, clear its defending CAP, and hold while it attacks a military radar; reserve fighters may launch from a second base as neutral traffic diverts along the coast. Do not cross the no-fly line; success requires the package to attack and egress before an 80-minute recovery. Deliver the .miz and section briefs, validating timing and triggers.
----

==== Fighter Sweep

[source,text]
----
Generate a two-player F-14B fighter sweep over afternoon Syria. Enter a mountain corridor ahead of the main strike to draw out MiG-21Bis patrols and a later MiG-29S response, while friendly M-2000Cs cover another axis and a neutral transport departs nearby. Open a 20-minute corridor, respect the pursuit line and recovery fuel, and return around 75 minutes; generate and check the .miz, routes, and objectives.
----

==== Escort

[source,text]
----
Build a four-ship full-fidelity M-2000C escort over Sinai for two low-level Mirage F1s striking a military depot beneath broken cloud. AWACS and tanker support are time-limited, interceptors threaten ingress, and a second group may pursue from an alternate field on egress; villages and civilian routes remain protected. Let players choose close or forward escort, but require the strikers to attack and escape; validate formation survival and 90-minute recovery in the .miz.
----

==== Airbase Defense

[source,text]
----
Generate a 1970s F-5E FC airbase defense in the Mariana Islands at dusk. Player and AI wingman start hot on alert as friendly MiG-21Bis aircraft return low on fuel and attackers approach the runway and fuel area at sea level; base guns engage, but the first bombing pass may damage the runway. Identify the raiders, defend flight and base, and divert if damage demands it; validate the .miz for damage, outcome logic, and a 55-minute limit.
----

==== Fleet Air Defense

[source,text]
----
Build an F-14A fleet defense over Kola in polar daylight. A two-ship holds carrier CAP as E-2 reports bombers approaching on two axes; the surface group and another F-14 flight cover only part of the threat, and a reconnaissance contact may lure players away before the main raid. Prioritize distant interception of launch aircraft and keep ships as the last layer; validate leakers and carrier recovery within 90 minutes in the .miz.
----

==== OCA Against Aircraft

[source,text]
----
Generate a four-ship F-16C offensive-counter-air mission on Nevada at night, framed as a fictional high-intensity exercise. Suppress airborne alert fighters, military parking areas, and runway operations before later sorties form, while friendly JF-17s handle SEAD and AWACS and tanker windows shape timing; a neutral business jet remains in the shared civilian sector. Keep civilian facilities off limits and withdraw before mission minute 85; confirm feasible objectives, then deliver the .miz, role brief, and validation record.
----

=== Air-to-Ground Operations

==== Preplanned CAS

[source,text]
----
Build winter-dawn preplanned CAS for two Su-25Ts over the Caucasus. Friendly troops hold a mountain pass against two confirmed armored groups and an artillery observer; a ground commander defines the safety line and attack window as a Mi-8 casualty flight departs and short-range defenses move with the convoy. Choose the attack order, stay behind the fire-coordination line, and return after stopping the main threat; validate the ground battle and 70-minute recovery in the .miz.
----

==== On-Call CAS

[source,text]
----
Generate two-player AH-64D on-call CAS on Sinai during a hot afternoon. Hold until a friendly reconnaissance team calls for help and a JTAC progressively supplies target description and clearance; hostile vehicles move along a village edge among friendlies and civilian traffic as dust reduces identification range. Fire only after identification, permit refusal of unsafe attacks, and cover the team's withdrawal; validate clearance and the 65-minute timeline in the .miz.
----

==== BAI

[source,text]
----
Build four-ship F-16C battlefield interdiction under an overcast Caucasus sky. Armored reinforcements approach the front through two valleys, reconnaissance updates are intermittent, friendly artillery will block one junction, and ordinary traffic shares the area. Choose the ambush, but never chase dispersed columns into the friendly contact zone; generate a 75-minute .miz and validate road movement and scoring.
----

==== Armed Reconnaissance

[source,text]
----
Generate OH-58D armed reconnaissance in the Syrian hills. Player and AI wingman launch at dawn to find suspected missile teams and supply vehicles from incomplete ground-patrol and friendly UAV reports; hostile crews may relocate or use unarmed vehicles as decoys. Prioritize finding, marking, and reporting, engaging only confirmed and cleared targets; validate discovery, authorization, and alternate egress in the 60-minute .miz.
----

==== Pinpoint Strike

[source,text]
----
Build a two-ship JF-17 pinpoint strike over the cloudy Mariana Islands. Cold-start from an island base to attack a military communications center at a city edge, with AWACS support, short-range defenses, and an evacuating civilian convoy on a nearby road. Use a verified weapon and attack direction, then decide whether backup equipment merits a second pass; deliver the 75-minute .miz, illustrated brief, and validation results.
----

==== Deep Strike

[source,text]
